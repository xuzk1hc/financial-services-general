# Investment Framework

Use this file when generating hotspot rankings, evidence labels, and investable analysis.

## Evidence Tiers

- `A 官方/监管`: exchange公告, company filing, official website, regulator notice, government policy document, earnings call transcript, verified order/contract disclosure.
- `B 可信媒体/数据`: reputable financial media, recognized data provider, broker report with identifiable source, exchange market data, well-sourced industry report.
- `C 群内可追溯`: group message includes a screenshot, link, named source, or clear channel-check context, but has not been independently verified.
- `D 传言/情绪`: unsourced rumor, "朋友说",喊单, pure price excitement, joke, or repeated claim without new evidence.

Never upgrade a claim just because many people repeat it.

## Hotspot Score

Score qualitatively from 1 to 5 or rank without numeric scores when data is thin. Consider:

- breadth: number of independent speakers or platforms
- recurrence: repeated mentions across time, not copy-paste spam
- recency: discussion closer to the requested window matters more
- specificity: concrete catalyst, date, order, product,政策, earnings item, or measurable event
- source quality: official/public source beats channel chatter
- market linkage: clear connection to price action, sector rotation, liquidity, or fund flow
- disagreement: strong informed disagreement may raise follow-up importance but lower confidence

## Theme Construction

Convert messages into investable themes by asking:

1. What changed today?
2. Who benefits directly?
3. Who benefits indirectly through the supply chain?
4. Is the catalyst already priced in, still developing, or likely false?
5. What public evidence would confirm or kill the thesis?

For each theme, separate:

- catalyst
- beneficiary chain
- mentioned tickers
- evidence tier
- risk or disproof path
- watch signal for tomorrow or this week

## Noise Filters

Down-rank:

- pure喊单 without reason
- copied market rumors without original source
- repeated screenshots with no source date
- discussions driven only by intraday price movement
- unrelated macro arguments that do not connect to a ticker, sector, or asset class

Flag early:

- small-cap liquidity risk
- regulatory/compliance risk
- earnings restatement or filing delay
- highly leveraged/options/crypto exposure
- crowded trades and after-hours/premarket quote anomalies

## Verification Rules

If the user asks for "今天", "最新", current prices, breaking catalysts, or official confirmation, verify with live sources before treating the claim as fact. Cite sources in the final answer.

If browsing or data access is unavailable, state that the report is based only on the provided chat messages and label all external claims as unverified.
