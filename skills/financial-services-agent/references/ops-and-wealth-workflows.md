# Operations And Wealth Workflows

Use this reference for wealth management, advisor workflows, client reporting, financial planning, tax-loss harvesting, fund administration, reconciliations, valuation review, month-end close, statement audit, and KYC/onboarding.

## Wealth Management

### Client Review

Use for periodic advisor/client meeting preparation.

Include:

- Portfolio performance versus benchmark and objectives.
- Asset allocation versus target.
- Contributions, withdrawals, and major transactions.
- Risk exposures and concentration.
- Tax considerations for advisor review.
- Life-event or goal updates if provided.
- Talking points and follow-ups.

Do not make suitability claims without enough client context. Frame as advisor review material.

### Financial Plan

Use for retirement, education, estate, cash-flow, or goal-based planning.

Include:

- Inputs and assumptions.
- Cash-flow projection.
- Goal funding status.
- Scenario analysis.
- Risks: market, longevity, inflation, liquidity, taxes.
- Open data needed for a qualified professional.

Label tax/legal assumptions as review items.

### Portfolio Rebalance

Use for allocation drift and tax-aware trade planning.

Include:

- Current allocation.
- Target allocation.
- Drift by asset class/security.
- Proposed rebalance amounts for review.
- Tax lots, gains/losses, wash sale constraints if available.
- Risk and liquidity notes.

Do not submit trades.

### Client Report / Investment Proposal

Use for client-facing summaries.

Standards:

- Plain language.
- Clear tables and charts.
- Source and period labels.
- Avoid jargon unless audience expects it.
- Keep recommendations framed for advisor approval.

### Tax-Loss Harvesting

Use for identifying candidate losses and replacement exposure.

Check:

- Unrealized gains/losses by tax lot.
- Wash sale windows.
- Portfolio exposure changes.
- Short-term vs long-term treatment.
- Client restrictions.

Stage candidates for tax/advisor review; do not give final tax advice.

## Fund Administration And Finance Ops

### Valuation Review

Use for GP packages, private holdings, valuation marks, and LP reporting prep.

Review:

- Valuation date and methodology.
- Source documents.
- Comparable companies/transactions.
- Discount rates, multiples, and calibration.
- Prior mark movement.
- Support for material changes.
- Open questions and exceptions.

Stage findings for valuation committee or reviewer approval.

### GL Reconciliation

Use for finding breaks between ledger, subledger, bank, custodian, or report systems.

Output:

- Reconciliation scope and period.
- Source systems.
- Matched items.
- Breaks and likely root cause.
- Owner and next action.
- Aging and materiality.
- Sign-off status.

Do not post journal entries; stage proposed entries for approval.

### Month-End Close

Use for accruals, roll-forwards, variance commentary, and close packages.

Include:

- Close checklist status.
- Accrual and adjustment support.
- Roll-forward tables.
- Actual vs budget/forecast variance.
- Open items.
- Required approvals.

### Statement Audit

Use for auditing LP/client/account statements before distribution.

Check:

- Beginning and ending balances.
- Contributions/distributions.
- Fees and expenses.
- Capital account movements.
- NAV and ownership percentages.
- Footnotes, dates, and entity names.
- Tie-out to source systems.

Flag exceptions and do not distribute statements.

## KYC And Onboarding

Use for onboarding package review, document parsing, and rules-grid evaluation.

Process:

1. Identify entity/person type and jurisdiction.
2. Inventory required documents.
3. Extract fields from submitted documents.
4. Evaluate completeness against the relevant rules grid.
5. Flag inconsistencies, expired documents, sanctions/PEP/adverse media follow-up needs if tools are available.
6. Produce gaps, evidence, and escalation list.

Do not approve onboarding. Stage results for compliance review.

## Controls

For all operations workflows:

- Preserve original source documents.
- Track transformations and assumptions.
- Separate confirmed facts from suspected issues.
- Use materiality thresholds when provided.
- Record human owner, reviewer, and sign-off status when available.
