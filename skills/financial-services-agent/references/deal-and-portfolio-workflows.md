# Deal And Portfolio Workflows

Use this reference for investment banking, private equity, deal execution, diligence, IC materials, and portfolio monitoring.

## Banking Deliverables

### One-Pager / Strip Profile

Use for pitch books, corp dev target profiles, buyer prep, or BD meeting prep.

Include:

- Company snapshot.
- Business description.
- Key financials and KPIs.
- Ownership and leadership.
- Products, segments, customers, geography.
- Trading comps or transaction comps if public/relevant.
- Recent news, M&A, financing, and strategic rationale.

Keep it concise and source-backed. Do not over-explain familiar companies unless the audience needs it.

### Pitch Deck

Use for client pitches, process updates, buyer outreach, and strategic alternatives.

Typical flow:

- Situation and objective.
- Market backdrop.
- Company positioning.
- Strategic alternatives or recommendation framing for human review.
- Valuation summary.
- Buyer/investor landscape.
- Process timeline and next steps.

Use firm templates when available.

### CIM

Use for confidential information memorandum drafts.

Typical sections:

- Executive summary.
- Investment highlights.
- Company overview.
- Market opportunity.
- Products/services.
- Customers and sales model.
- Operations.
- Management team.
- Historical and projected financials.
- Risks and diligence questions.

Flag missing company-provided data rather than fabricating detail.

### Teaser

Use for anonymous buyer outreach.

Rules:

- Remove identifying details unless user permits disclosure.
- Preserve enough specificity to qualify interest.
- Use ranges where exact figures would reveal identity.
- Keep source and confidentiality notes in working files, not necessarily in outward teaser text.

### Buyer List

Use for strategic/financial buyer universe.

Include:

- Buyer name and type.
- Strategic rationale.
- Relevant acquisitions or investments.
- Size/capability fit.
- Geography/customer/channel fit.
- Likelihood or priority score with stated criteria.
- Conflicts, antitrust, financing, and relationship notes.

## Private Equity Workflows

### Deal Sourcing

Use for opportunity discovery and outreach prep.

Output:

- Target universe with inclusion/exclusion criteria.
- Company profiles.
- Ownership/funding status.
- Signals: growth, hiring, product launches, customer wins, financing, M&A.
- Outreach angle.
- Data confidence and gaps.

### Deal Screening

Use for inbound CIM/teaser quick pass.

Assess:

- Sector fit.
- Business quality.
- Revenue quality and growth.
- Margin profile.
- Customer concentration.
- Market size and competition.
- Valuation expectation.
- Diligence red flags.

Return pass/watch/pass-with-issues/reject framing for human review.

### Diligence Checklist

Organize by workstream:

- Commercial.
- Financial.
- Accounting/quality of earnings.
- Tax.
- Legal.
- Technology/product.
- Cybersecurity/data privacy.
- HR/people.
- Operations.
- ESG/regulatory where relevant.

For each item, include owner, source, status, and open issues.

### Unit Economics

Use for SaaS, marketplaces, fintech, consumer, and recurring-revenue businesses.

Analyze:

- ARR/MRR, cohort growth, retention, churn.
- Gross retention and net dollar retention.
- CAC, CAC payback, LTV/CAC.
- Gross margin and contribution margin.
- Revenue quality and concentration.
- Cohort behavior over time.

Do not force SaaS metrics onto non-SaaS businesses.

### Returns Analysis

Use for PE underwriting and exit sensitivities.

Include:

- Entry valuation.
- Operating case.
- Leverage and debt paydown.
- Exit valuation.
- IRR and MOIC.
- Sensitivities for exit multiple, EBITDA, leverage, and holding period.

### IC Memo

Expected sections:

- Recommendation for committee review.
- Investment summary.
- Company and market overview.
- Thesis and value creation plan.
- Financial profile and model summary.
- Valuation and returns.
- Diligence findings.
- Risks and mitigants.
- Open items and decision asks.

Be explicit about what is verified, assumed, and still pending.

### Portfolio Monitoring

Track:

- KPI dashboard.
- Actual vs budget.
- Rolling forecast.
- Revenue and margin bridge.
- Cash/liquidity.
- Covenant or reporting requirements.
- Value creation initiatives.
- Risks and required actions.

## AI Readiness Review

Use for assessing a portfolio company's AI opportunity.

Assess:

- Data availability and quality.
- Process automation candidates.
- Customer-facing use cases.
- Internal productivity use cases.
- Security and compliance constraints.
- Build/buy/partner options.
- ROI, cost, and implementation risk.

Tie recommendations to business value and operating constraints.
